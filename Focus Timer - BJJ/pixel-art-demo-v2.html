Technical Specifications

Canvas & Rendering

  • Base canvas: 48×64 px (per sprite)
  • Render scale: 3× nearest-neighbor (NO anti-aliasing)
  • Display size: 144×192 px on screen
  • Background: Transparent PNG only
  • Anchor point: Feet to bottom-center
  • Color mode: Indexed palette (swappable)

Why 48×64?

  • Belt stripes must be visible (4 white marks)
  • Gi collar detail needs clarity
  • Gear differences must read clearly
  • Facial expressions require space
  • 32×48 loses critical detail for progression system

Animation Frame Counts

  • Idle: 4 frames (breathing loop)
  • Training: 6 frames (drill motion)
  • Celebration: 8 frames (plays once)
  • Discouraged: 4 frames (loops)
  • Rank-up: 10 frames (special ceremony)

File Naming Convention

  • layer_body_base_f01.png
  • layer_hair_back_style03_f01.png
  • layer_gear_gi_neutral_f01.png
  • layer_belt_white_f01.png
  • layer_belt_blue_f01.png
  • layer_accessories_earring_f01.png

Palette Swapping

  • Skin tone: 6 palettes × 4 shades each
  • Hair color: 10 palettes × 4 shades
  • Eye color: 6 palettes × 3 shades
  • Gear colors: Unlocked via progression
  • Belt color: Rank-based (not user-selectable)

Belt Progression

  • White → Blue → Purple → Brown → Black → Coral
  • Each belt has 4 stripes (white tape marks)
  • Black belt has 10 degrees (red bars)
  • Coral belt is ultimate unlock (split red/white)
  • Stripes must be visible as distinct pixel marks

Art Style Reference

  • JRPG pixel art (Final Fantasy VI, Chrono Trigger)
  • 4-shade color ramps for depth
  • Strong outlines for clarity
  • Readable silhouettes at base scale
  • Martial arts realism (not fantasy/sci-fi)
  • Clean, professional sprite work
