# Market Research — Small-Business AI Consulting (2026-11-02)

Purpose: figure out what "AI consulting for small businesses" actually means as a product, what solo operators sell, and whether social media is required, **before** building the ryAIn Consulting site. Findings, not decisions. Nothing here is locked. Promote items to `business-plan.md` once Ryan decides.

Source quality warning: most pages about "AI consulting pricing" are written by people selling AI services or "start an AI agency" courses. The numbers below are marketing-grade. The independent survey data (Goldman Sachs, SCORE) is more reliable.

---

## 1. The demand is real, but it's for *help*, not for "AI"

- 76% of small businesses use AI in some form, but only **14% have it built into their core operations**. 73% say they'd benefit from help implementing it. (Goldman Sachs 10KSB, 2026)
- The top barriers are cost, not understanding the technology, and not seeing a clear ROI. 74% of "explorers" would adopt if they saw clearer ROI evidence. (Reimagine Main Street; UK surveys)
- Owners want automation **but want a human available**. (Simply Business 2026)

**What this means:** nobody buys "AI." Owners buy a fix for a specific pain ("I miss calls," "quotes take me an hour") that happens to use AI. Sell outcomes; mention the AI as *how* you do it. That fits Ryan's "don't hide that we use AI" stance.

## 2. What solo / small AI consultants actually sell

These are ranked by how often they show up as a real, productized offer for *local* businesses:

| Offer | What it is | Typical price (vendor-reported) | Fit for Ryan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Missed-call text-back** | Auto-texts anyone whose call goes unanswered, then routes the reply into a booking link or inbox | Often bundled into a $150–$300/mo package | ★★★ Simple, easy to demo, ROI is obvious |
| **AI receptionist / voice agent** | AI answers the phone, handles FAQs, books appointments, escalates to a human | ~$300–$600/mo per location | ★★ Upsell later; more support burden |
| **Lead follow-up and review requests** | Automated reply to form/DM inquiries, reminders, "leave us a Google review" texts after a visit | Bundled with the above | ★★★ Pairs naturally with GBP work |
| **AI audit / "where can AI save you time"** | Fixed-price session: map workflows, deliver a prioritized list | $500–$2,500 one-time | ★★ Good entry product; needs credibility first |
| **Hands-on AI setup and training** | Set up ChatGPT/Claude for *their* business (templates, custom assistants for quotes, emails, posts) | $300–$1,500 | ★★ Can't compete with *free* generic workshops (see below), so it must be specific to their business |
| **Custom builds** (internal tools, small apps) | One-off projects | $1,500–$15,000 | ★ Service line 4; later, case by case |
| **Retainers** | Ongoing tweaks, "own the next workflow" | $500–$5,000/mo | The long-term goal; needs a defined deliverable or it churns |

**Key observations:**
- **The generic "AI chatbot for your business" pitch is saturated.** What still works is specializing in one type of business or one specific problem.
- **Many "AI agencies" are mostly reselling a white-label platform** (GoHighLevel, Vendasta) with setup on top. Tradeoff: fast to launch, but the platform's monthly cost ($97–$297+/mo) eats into the margin at small-business prices.
- **Free competition:** the U.S. Chamber, Google ("Small Business B(AI)sics"), and SBDCs run *free* AI workshops for owners. Don't sell generic AI training. Instead, **teach at one for free as lead generation.**
- **Realistic pace:** solo operators without an existing network land roughly **3–6 real engagements in year one** (an agency's own estimate). The "$20k/month" claims come from course sellers.
- **Churn:** retainers churn when the value becomes invisible. Send a monthly "what this did for you" report (for example, "14 missed calls recovered, 3 bookings").
- **Compliance note:** automated business texting in the US requires A2P 10DLC registration, and SMS falls under TCPA consent rules. It's not hard, but it has to be done right. Platforms usually handle the registration.

## 4. Do you need social media?

**Short answer: not to get your first clients. Yes as proof, kept minimal.**

- Referral study (SCORE): **verbal referrals convert to sales 32% of the time; social media referrals convert 1%.** Social media generates a lot of *awareness* but converts poorly.
- Local owners choose vendors they've met or that a peer recommended. Then they **check you out online** before replying. Social media's real job is to pass that check, not to bring in leads.
- An Instagram account with 0 followers won't produce leads for months. It still helps as a **portfolio**: before/after site screenshots and short "here's what I built for X" posts.
- **YouTube long-form** is expensive in time and isn't where tattoo shop owners find vendors. It may be worth doing later for goal #1 (job security / learning in public), but it's a weak lead source for local clients.
- **Your own Google Business Profile** probably matters more than Instagram for a local service business, and you already sell that skill.

Minimum viable presence: website + GBP + Instagram used as a portfolio (1–2 posts/week, repurposed from real work). Skip YouTube for now.

## 5. What's actually working for client acquisition (solo, local)

1. **Referrals from existing clients.** Ask directly, and maybe offer an incentive. The founding-client offer already covers this.
2. **Niche clustering.** Once you're "the tattoo shop website/AI person," one happy shop refers others. Peer referral is the 32% channel.
3. **Walk in / show a demo.** Missed-call text-back demos in 60 seconds: call their number, don't answer, and their phone gets a text.
4. **Teach a free workshop** at the local chamber or library, and leave with a list of owners.
5. **Footer credit** ("Site by ryAIn Consulting") on every client site.
6. Cold DMs/email: the most crowded channel, so it works worst.

## 6. Honest business viability

- **$50/mo websites alone:** 20 clients ≈ $1,000/mo. A real side income, but capped, and support load grows with every client. Good as a **foot in the door** and as a trust builder.
- **The money in this space is the next step:** lead-capture automation at $100–$300/mo on top of a site you already host, or fixed-price projects. The best clients are the ones whose site you already run.
- **Constraint:** you have a day job. Avoid offers that need you to answer phones live or respond within minutes (a self-managed AI receptionist could). Favor simple systems the owner can keep running without you.

## 7. Suggested ladder (proposal — not decided)

1. **Website, handled** ($50/mo, existing offer). Lead offer.
2. **Never miss an inquiry** (~$100–$200/mo add-on): missed-call text-back, auto-reply to inquiries, review requests, monthly report.
3. **AI help for your business** (fixed-price, on request): audit + hands-on setup.
4. Custom builds: case by case, not advertised heavily.
